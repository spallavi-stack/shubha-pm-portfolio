Not yet started. A predictive outreach coordination platform for homeless services NGOs and city agencies: combining NWS forecast data (heat, cold, flood, hurricane) with known encampment locations and HUD's public point-in-time count data, so outreach teams can pre-position blankets, water, and staff before a heat wave or cold snap, not after. Process

The same five steps, every case study.

Research the real problem first

Pick an industry, research what's broken for the target user today, using real market/regulatory data, before framing a product solution.

Build personas & run interviews

Ground personas in that real research; run synthetic interviews with them to pressure-test assumptions before roadmapping.

Prioritize with a defensible framework

ICE, RICE, Value vs. Effort - chosen deliberately per project, every score tied to a specific persona, JTBD, interview finding, or pressing regulatory update.

Prototype it

A clickable, coded prototype of the MVP flow (no wireframes unless necessary), so the product is something stakeholders can actually touch and feel, before making decisions.

Prove it's buildable and trackable

Layering in feasibility notes, adoption/PMF tracking, and success metrics on top of the case studies that warrant it.
