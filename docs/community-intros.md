## Work on Climate

Hi everyone, I'm Shubha, a product manager in energy and climate tech, based in Berlin between trips. Since November I've been on the road: volunteering at a wildlife rescue centre in Indonesia, a dog shelter in Thailand, and a run of pet-sits across Mexico, building climate product case studies from wherever I land.

The work behind that: dynamic electricity pricing at Tibber, then smart PV and battery control, EV smart charging and a solar calculator at ison, a corporate venture of the utility LichtBlick.
