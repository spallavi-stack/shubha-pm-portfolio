# Fund the Future

A campaign-building and fundraising platform for grassroots climate-adaptation organisations. An organisation enters who it is and what it needs money for, and gets a shareable campaign page with a working donation path. Pilot market is the Philippines.

Fictional case study. Market research is real and cited; the organisations, people, campaigns and donations are synthetic, and no money moves at any point.

## What is in here

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The case study page |
| `prototype.html` | The clickable prototype, self-contained, both flows and both sides |
| `docs/` | Markdown source paired with rendered HTML |
| `assets/` | Images and screenshots |

## The documents

**Public, rendered by `scripts/build_docs.py fund-the-future` and linked from the case study page:**

- `product-brief.md` — opportunity assessment against the frozen template
- `market-selection.md` — five criteria, and why the Philippines
- `personas.md` — seven, across organisations, donors and one institutional visitor
- `jobs-to-be-done.md` — nine jobs, forces analysis, job map
- `synthetic-interviews.md` — six invented interviews used as a design tool
- `roadmap.md` — phase 0 is the research that decides whether to build
- `user-stories.md` — four epics with checkable acceptance criteria
- `technical-feasibility.md` — the architecture a real build would need

**Internal, never rendered and never linked publicly:**

- `HANDOVER.md` — read this first in a new session. Decisions, corrections and the consolidated open worklist
- `scope.md` — what this pass covers and what it deliberately does not
- `grounding-research.md` — sixteen sections, every claim tagged Fact, Inference or Assumption
- `design-brief.md` — visual direction, the onboarding model and the seven-theme library

## Reading order

`HANDOVER.md`, then `scope.md`, then `market-selection.md`, then `product-brief.md`, then `design-brief.md`. `grounding-research.md` is the full backing material behind all of them.
